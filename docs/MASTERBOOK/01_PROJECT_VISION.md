# CHAPTER 01 — PROJECT VISION & PLATFORM ROADMAP

## Mission Statement
Zahdan Kamal Sentosa (ZKS) is an industrial central kitchen and corporate catering operator delivering thousands of meals daily across enterprise manufacturing plants, commercial towers, healthcare facilities, and executive events.

The platform is evolving from a customer-facing public profile into a full-scale Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform designed to automate, audit, and optimize end-to-end catering logistics.

---

## Planned Core Modules

1. **Company Website & Public Portal**: Public identity, corporate credibility, interactive industrial menus, order locks, and proof-of-payment token uploads.
2. **Authentication & Identity**: Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authorization, granular permission matrix (Superadmin, Warehouse Staff, Kitchen Supervisor, Procurement Officer, Auditor).
3. **Executive Dashboard**: Unified command center visualizing operational SLAs, cash flow, stock health, and daily dispatch counts.
4. **Inventory Management**: Digital item passport, multi-category raw materials, batch traceability, stock movement audit trail, physical stock opname.
5. **Warehouse Management**: Central cold storage, dry storage, loading dock receiving, quality inspection, internal transfers.
6. **Procurement**: Intelligent purchase requests with automated low-stock triggering, multi-vendor purchase orders, PO lifecycle tracking.
7. **Supplier Management**: Approved vendor lists (AVL), performance scoring, lead-time tracking, compliance certifications.
8. **Production & Kitchen**: Central kitchen work orders, Bill of Materials (BOM), recipe scaling, prep station batch scheduling.
9. **Distribution & Dispatch**: Fleet dispatch routing, insulated thermal box tagging, dispatch checklist, digital delivery receipts.
10. **Finance & Accounting**: Invoicing, AP/AR, cost of goods sold (COGS) variance tracking, corporate credit terms.
11. **Human Resources**: Food-handler health cards, chef certification tracking, shift scheduling, kitchen station capacity.
12. **Reporting & Business Intelligence**: Cross-module analytics, food waste audits, consumption velocity, profitability per menu item.
13. **AI Assistant & Automation**: Predictive inventory restocking, anomaly detection in stock movements, automated recipe grammage adjustments.
