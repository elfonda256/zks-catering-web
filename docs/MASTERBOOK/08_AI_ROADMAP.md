# CHAPTER 08 — AI ROADMAP & INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION

## Overview
The ZKS Enterprise ERP platform incorporates artificial intelligence as an operational co-pilot for kitchen managers, inventory controllers, and corporate executives.

## Phased Roadmap

### Phase 1: Predictive Inventory Restocking
- Forecast weekly demand using historical event schedules, weather signals, and seasonality.
- Calculate dynamic buffer stock levels for perishable vegetables and poultry.

### Phase 2: Anomaly Detection in Stock & Cost Auditing
- Real-time detection of suspicious inventory write-offs or unexplained variance during stock opname.
- Automated price benchmark comparison across suppliers for core commodities (rice, cooking oil, chicken).

### Phase 3: Kitchen Menu Engineering & Waste Minimization
- Smart cross-utilization recommendations (e.g., repurposing surplus roasted meats into stir-fry batches).
- Real-time carbon footprint and nutritional balancing assistant.

### Phase 4: Executive Natural Language Query Interface
- Conversational querying: *"What was our food cost variance for industrial clients in Plant A this month?"*
- Automated audit executive summaries and PDF report compilation.
